## ICD-10-CM terminology

ICD-10-CM source materials are provided by the CDC's National Center for Health
Statistics (CDC/NCHS). Official materials are available without charge from
[CDC/NCHS](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/icd/icd-10-cm/index.html).
Downloaded archives, guidelines, terminology text and generated catalogs are
not relicensed under this project's Apache license. Preserve upstream notices,
source wording, release identities and provenance when using those materials.
Search ranking, extracted relationships and validation output are generated by
this independent software, not official or revised coding guidance.

[CDC's reuse policy](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html) says **most**,
not all, agency materials are public domain. It includes attribution,
nonendorsement and free-source requirements and warns about third-party works
and international protection. Do not assume all CDC downloads are public domain.
WHO ICD-10 is distinct from the US clinical modification, ICD-10-CM; see the
[WHO licensing FAQ](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/publishing-policies/copyright/who-faq-licensing-icd-10.pdf).
This project does not establish worldwide redistribution rights for every
source archive or derived catalog. Check publisher terms and obtain NCHS or
appropriate legal clarification before redistributing these assets.

This independent project is not endorsed by CDC, NCHS, CMS, HHS, WHO or the
United States Government. No agency name, logo or trademark rights are granted.

## Python dependencies

The official MCP SDK, HTTP/XML/database-related libraries and their transitive
dependencies retain their own licenses and notices. Exact runtime versions and
hashes are in [the core lock](requirements/core.lock) and [uv.lock](uv.lock).
They are installed separately, not relicensed by this repository. If distributing
an image, bundled environment or vendored code, inspect those exact distributions
and include their applicable licenses and notices. This file is not that inventory.

## Optional OpenMed and model assets

Optional OpenMed software, model weights, tokenizers and configuration files
remain subject to their publishers' terms at the exact revisions used.
Publisher-declared Apache licensing does not independently clear training-data
rights. Existing BC5CDR/Nemotron-PII provenance uncertainties remain unresolved;
see [the local OpenMed guide](docs/local-openmed.md).

Downloaded catalogs, source archives, environments and model assets are ignored
and are not part of the project's software packages. Core code/phrase lookup
does not depend on OpenMed, model weights or application-side LLM calls.
